### Journal #2

Today is 11/28/2026.

I've actually realised that recreating the base part and the part attached to it (Part 1) using FreeCAD wasn't that bad after all. FreeCAD is pretty easy to learn, and I have to say I was quite surprised by that because I thought it would be much more difficult.

I think it's partly because I already have some experience with Blender. I mean, I managed to make my doughnut pretty well in Blender before, so I feel like that helped me gain some useful skills that I could carry over to FreeCAD. And the best part is that FreeCAD has a feature that allows you to use it in a way that's more similar to Blender, almost like a Blender-style CAD workspace or something. That definitely helped too, I guess.
